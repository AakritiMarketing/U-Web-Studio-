Questions people ask

Do I pay when I send my request?

No. Sending your request is free. We'll message you on WhatsApp to confirm your choices and the final price, then take a deposit before we start.

How long does it take?

Simple sites are usually ready in about a week. Online shops with many products take two to three weeks, mostly depending on how quickly product details and photos are ready.

Can I add features later?

Yes. Start small and add payments, a chatbot or anything else whenever you're ready, at the same price shown in the builder.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes. Add the Admin dashboard feature and you get your own login to change products, prices, photos and text, and to see orders or bookings.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. Your domain and any platform accounts are registered in your name, and you can take them anywhere.

What do I need to accept card payments in the UAE?

A valid trade license that covers selling online. The payment company approves your account, which can take one to three weeks, so we start that on day one.

What does "behind the scenes" mean on some features?

Some features, like Google search setup or AI search readiness, don't change how the page looks. They work in the background so Google and AI assistants understand and recommend your site.
